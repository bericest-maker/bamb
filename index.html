WHAT CHANGED (v8.5 — this round)

keep placingSHIFT-drag runbackpack stays open auto rarity sortworm spawns midboss slam custom boss HPno unit collisionno drowning
  • 🧱 KEEP PLACING. Drop a building and the next one from your backpack stack is handed straight to the cursor — build a whole row without reopening the backpack. It stops (and tells you) when the stack is empty.
  • 🧱 SHIFT + DRAG = PLACE A RUN. Hold SHIFT, press and drag out an area: every footprint is previewed live (green where it fits, red where it's taken, floating at the stack level it would land on) with a N × item label — and they all go down when you release. It stops at the last one you own, and it spreads them side by side instead of stacking them.
  • 📦 THE BACKPACK STAYS OPEN while you place — click the next card to switch building.
  • 📋 AUTO-SORT. The SHOP, the BACKPACK and both ADMIN lists are sorted automatically: best rarity at the top, worst at the bottom (crates first in the backpack).
  • 🐍 THE WORM SURFACES IN THE MIDDLE (the CITY island) instead of a random base — and it fights: every 4.5s it SLAMS every enemy unit within 210px (45 damage) and crushes enemy buildings within 260px (130 damage), with a shockwave you can see.
  • 🐍 ADMIN → CUSTOM BOSS HP. Type 5000 / 250K / 1.5M and press SET HP — it sets the live worm and every boss that spawns later. Leave it empty and press SET HP to go back to the default.
  • 🐜 NO MORE UNIT COLLISION. Troops never block each other now — they only drift apart a little when they end up on top of each other (the "don't touch" nudge), soldiers and ships alike. Columns no longer freeze on bridges.
  • 🐜 NO MORE DROWNING. A soldier that ends up in the water is put straight back on the nearest shore.

WHAT CHANGED (v8.4 — previous round)

stackingunlimited heightbackpack stacks open 1 / 5 / 10 / ALLrobux shop fixedcrate results list
  • 🧱 BUILDINGS STACK — AS HIGH AS YOU LIKE. Aim at a building you already own and the next one lands on top of it instead of being refused. Each floor is lifted 24px and drawn above the one below, and the ghost shows dashed drop-legs plus a LEVEL n label so you always know which floor you're building.
  • Every floor works on its own — 4 barracks stacked 4 high train 4 recruits. Turrets, hospitals, depots and money buildings all stack too. Clicking a stack picks the crate you actually aimed at (not the one underneath), and bots still spread out across the island first — they only climb a pile when your island is full.
  • 📦 THE BACKPACK STACKS. Identical items are now one card with an ×N badge instead of forty separate cards. Placing a building takes one out of the stack; buying or winning more adds to it.
  • 🎁 OPEN CRATES IN BULK. Click a crate stack and choose 1 / 5 / 10 / ALL. It rolls them all at once and then lists everything you got — rarest first, with ×counts and rarity colours.
  • 💎 THE ROBUX SHOP WORKS — the tab used to call a function that didn't exist and threw an error. It now sells Standard / Elite / Premium crates for cash, 1 or 10 at a time.
  • Old saves are safe: buildings that now overlap are stacked instead of being returned to the backpack, and your old per-item backpack is folded into stacks.

WHAT CHANGED (v8 — previous round)

block modehide trees & decorworld ×2 no drive-by shootingmarch on the middlepermanent buildings enemy base grids hiddenlag fixes
  • 🧊 BLOCK MODE — ⚙ SETTINGS has two rows: UNIT GRAPHICS and BUILDING GRAPHICS, each Normal or Blocks. Blocks turns every troop into a plain rectangle exactly its sprite’s size and every building into its footprint rectangle — just the owner’s colour, nothing else (no shadows, no outlines). It skips the sprite drawing completely — the biggest frame-time win in the game.
  • 🌳 TREES & DECOR toggle — hide (and show again) trees, rocks, grass patches and the floating crystals. On a busy base that's ~350 tree draws per frame gone.
  • ✨ EFFECTS High/Low (was GRAPHICS MODE) — Low now also drops the ocean glints, the shipping-lane glow and draws coastlines with 28 instead of 72 segments.
  • 🚫 BUILDINGS ARE PERMANENT — nothing can destroy a building any more (raids, the Mecha Worm, artillery) and right-click no longer sells your own by accident. ⚙ SETTINGS → INDESTRUCTIBLE BUILDINGS off brings destructible bases and the 50% refund back.
  • 🗺️ THE WORLD IS TWICE AS BIG — 4800 → 9600 px, with the bases pushed out to a 2600px ring. Islands are ~1990px apart with wide ocean between them, so the march to the middle is a real journey and ships have room to sail. Shipping lanes, outposts, crystals and the city all scaled up.
  • 🎯 NO MORE DRIVE-BY SHOOTING — units only pick a fight when the enemy is CLOSE (210px while marching). Artillery used to shell your base from 430px away as it walked past. Units that HOLD a spot (garrisons, base defenders, idle troops) still shoot at full range.
  • 🏃 IDLE TROOPS MARCH ON THE MIDDLE — with no order your army heads for the CITY (then the nearest point you don't own) and fights whatever it meets on the way, instead of beelining for somebody's spawn.
  • 🕶️ ENEMY BASES: BUILDINGS + TROOPS ONLY — their build grids, dashed outlines and name labels are hidden. ⚙ SETTINGS → ENEMY BASE GRIDS brings the labels back if you want them.

HOW TO TRY THE NEW STUFF

  • LAGGING? ⚙ SETTINGS (left rail) → EFFECTS Low, UNIT GRAPHICS Blocks, BUILDING GRAPHICS Blocks, TREES & DECOR Off. Everything is live — no reload.
  • Press F1 → ADMIN for cash (+100M), any building (BP = to backpack) and any unit (SPAWN).
  • SHOP → UNITS → ⚓ NAVAL: buy a dock, place it near your coast; the ship launches into the water.
  • Buy a Bank and some money buildings → watch the 🏦 stored row fill up, then click a building to empty its safe.
  • ADMIN → RAID ALERT (or wait for a wave): your barracks/factories muster a free garrison.
  • Left rail → 📜 PATCHES shows this changelog inside the game.
  • TRY STACKING: F1 → ADMIN → BP any building twice, then place the second one directly on top of the first. Or press ADMIN → PILE to drop a 7-high tower in one click.
  • TRY BULK CRATES: 💎 ROBUX SHOP → BUY 10 premium crates → BACKPACK → click the crate card → ALL (10).
  • TRY THE BUILD FLOW: buy 10 solar panels → BACKPACK → click the card once → click, click, click (you keep placing). Or hold SHIFT and drag across your plot to lay a whole row at once.
  • TRY THE WORM: F1 → ADMIN → BOSS → SUMMON (it surfaces in the CITY now). Type a number in CUSTOM HP and press SET HP to make it as tanky as you like.
