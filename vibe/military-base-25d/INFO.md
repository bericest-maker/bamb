## ⚔️ Unit classes & damage

Classes (a unit can have several): 🪖 **LIGHT** · 🛡️ **ARMORED** · ✈️ **AIR** · 👻 **STEALTH** · ⚓ **NAVAL**

- **Damage** = `max(1, dmg × modifier − target armor)`.
- **Modifier** (`mods` vs the TARGET's classes): missing = ×1 · **0 = cannot damage / never targets** · multi-class target: any ×0 → 0, else the highest.
- **Air** flies straight over water. The **Drone** has `fly` but stays LIGHT (anti-air can't touch it).
- **Stealth** is invisible until: in combat, <70px, inside a unit's `detect`, or inside a friendly **Radar Station** (450px) / friendly detector (shared).
- Extras: `splash` (50% to others in radius) · `heal` (medic hp/s) · `bld` (× vs buildings: flak/AA 0.2, Saboteur 3) · `size` (troop-cap slots).

## 🧠 How the systems work

### Combat
- `findEnemyOf` picks the nearest enemy that is **visible** (`canSee`) and **hurtable** (modifier > 0). Shots every `rate` s; `splash` hits others at 50%.
- Buildings take `dmg × bld`. Turret buildings (`updateTurrets`) fire on their own with their own modifiers.
- Kill rewards go only to the player. God mode protects only YOUR units and buildings.

### Unit AI (`targetFor` → `updateUnit` → `stepUnit`)
- **Bots**: ordered units march the CITY (flow field / A*); others defend their base (chase threats <700px).
- **Player**: explicit order → ATTACK flag → nearest enemy point → nearest enemy building → plot centre. Garrisons hold their point.
- **Medics** don't fight: they follow orders and heal the most injured ally in range.
- **Movement**: air (and the Drone) flies straight; land uses the CITY flow field or A* (0.7s repath).

### Waves & boss
- First wave 90s, then every 120s: `3+wave` troops + `wave/2` tanks from a random surviving bot, troop types unlock with `WAVE_POOL`: wave 1+ rifle; wave 2+ scout, hinf; wave 3+ atv; wave 5+ humvee, rocket, heli; wave 8+ apc, sniper, flak, spectre; wave 12+ heavy, jet, cobra; wave 18+ phantom, mech.
- MECHA WORM: first at 180s, then every 300s after the last one dies. Kill = cash + Premium crate.

### Economy
- Income formula in `incomeRate`; the HUD shows the bonus % with a breakdown tooltip. Troop cap is counted in unit **size**.
- Rebirth: needs 5000·2.2^n power, +10% income forever; keeps golden buildings + Monuments.

### Save (localStorage `bmb25`, v4)
- Whole state; player units saved with `home` (garrison). v1/v2 → fresh map keeping progression; v3 → v4 (buildings outside the plot go back to the backpack, unknown types dropped, missing fields filled).

### Controls
- WASD/arrows pan · wheel zoom · drag select (Shift add) · Ctrl+click move · RMB cancel / sell 50% / assault · Esc · F1 admin · hover shop cards for full stats.

## 📖 Tutorial (4 steps)

1. Welcome, Commander. You've been given a plot on the island.  Pan with WASD (or arrows), zoom with the wheel. Your plot is the gold-bordered grid.
2. Open the SHOP (top) → PRODUCTION tab. Buy a Solar Array, then click your BACKPACK (left) and place it on the plot.  Buildings generate cash every second.
3. Buy a BARRACKS in the UNITS tab. It trains soldiers for free.  Drag to select units, Ctrl+click to give move orders — or just smash ATTACK bottom-left.
4. Money buildings store what they earn — click one to empty its safe, or wait for the payout. A BANK pays 5% interest on everything stored. While a raid is incoming your unit buildings train a free garrison.The ⚓ NAVAL tab builds ships: they sail the water lanes and shell the shore from the sea.The 8 plots are separate islands — bridges connect them. Seven enemy factions each have their own color and march on the CITY (the middle) by default. Capture points for production boosts (city = +20%) and the factions will fight over them.  Watch the top-right timer: raid waves and the MECHA WORM boss are coming. Rebirth for permanent +10% income.  Good luck, Commander. 🫡
